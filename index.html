Meet the Founder

Nuh Emrah Seren is a Senior Software Engineer, Technical Team Lead and technology entrepreneur with over 10 years of international experience in enterprise software development.

Throughout his career, he has contributed to large-scale, business-critical projects across banking, insurance, financial reporting and enterprise software systems. His professional experience includes working with global organizations such as General Electric (GE) and Accenture, contributing to complex technology solutions and taking on senior engineering and technical leadership responsibilities.

With extensive expertise in software architecture, enterprise integrations, backend development and technical team leadership, he founded AfroNextStep with a clear vision: to bring reliable, scalable and affordable technology solutions to businesses across Africa.

Through AfroNextStep and its growing portfolio of SaaS products, he aims to support digital transformation, encourage innovation and help African businesses grow through technology.
